SWARM WILDFIRE

The goal is:
(1) build a realistic environment of fire propagation and effect of water component on the fire dynamics
(2) build a model which learns to apply water on certain grid points at given timesteps. It can either be convolution-based or RL-based. 

First, I'd like to try to see if the pipeline works well on simple fire and water dynamics, before committing to more realistic setup. But the end goal is to use fire, water, and fire suppression / retardant dynamics as close as possible to reality, and also use constrained water action corresponding to different ways of action: either a plane, a swarm of drones, helicopters, etc. So if I can define from the start an infrastructure that could be extended well, that would be good.
